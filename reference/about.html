We are the official departmental technical collective of Electronics & Computer Science at Vivekanand Education Society's Institute of Technology (VESIT), Chembur. Founded to bridge theoretical curricula with hands-on silicon crafting, embedded systems, algorithmic problem solving, and full-stack software development. We organize practical workshops, competitive auctions, hardware exhibitions, and senior-fresher mentorships.

Hardware Meets Software

True engineering exists at the tactile junction of digital logic and physical circuits. We trace execution paths from raw transistor gates and microcontroller registers up through distributed compute abstractions.

[ PILLAR // 02 ] terminal

Real Workflows

No toy projects or synthetic coursework. Our workshops simulate production sprints, version-controlled firmware matrices, hardware debugging on logic analyzers, and stress-tested deployments under load.

Open to Every Year

FLUX eliminates academic hierarchy. First-year novices solder alongside fourth-year researchers. Peer mentorships are engineered so that collective technical memory is preserved across cohorts without friction.
