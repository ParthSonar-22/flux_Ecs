IoTA Archives Successfully Welcomes FE Cohort Across Labs 201, 203, and 215

On the 7th of September, the department convened its foundational welcoming exhibition. Guided under the esteemed auspices of Council In-charge Yogesh Pandit Sir and orchestrated by President Harsh with the senior executive committee, hundreds of First Year candidates crossed the threshold into applied silicon craftsmanship.

Laboratories 201, 203, and 215 were transformed into live deployment stations featuring bespoke hardware builds: autonomous obstacle-avoidance vehicles, dual-motor brawl combat arenas, FPGA signal generators, and edge AI/ML vision pipelines running directly on microprocessors.
